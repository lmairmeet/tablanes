# TabLanes privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 September 2026_

TabLanes does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.

## What the extension stores

Everything you put on the board — lane names, card titles, descriptions, links,
and any files or images you attach — is written to a SQLite database that lives
inside your browser profile on your own computer, alongside the attachment files
themselves. Nothing is uploaded.

## What the extension sends

Nothing. TabLanes makes no network requests of any kind. It contains no
analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no remote code:
every file it runs is shipped inside the extension package.

## Permissions

TabLanes requests a single permission, `unlimitedStorage`, so that the board is
not capped by the browser's default quota when you attach large images or files.
It grants no access to your browsing history, your tabs, or the pages you visit.

## Deleting your data

Removing TabLanes from `chrome://extensions` deletes its database and all
attachments along with it. There is no copy anywhere else.
